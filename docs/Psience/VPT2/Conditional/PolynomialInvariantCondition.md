## <a id="Psience.VPT2.Conditional.PolynomialInvariantCondition">PolynomialInvariantCondition</a> 

<div class="docs-source-link" markdown="1">
[[source](https://github.com/McCoyGroup/Psience/blob/master/Psience/VPT2/Conditional.py#L184)/
[edit](https://github.com/McCoyGroup/Psience/edit/master/Psience/VPT2/Conditional.py#L184?message=Update%20Docs)]
</div>

Retain equal McUtils polynomial/function invariants at both endpoints.

Variables of the McUtils functions refer to the supplied ``modes``.
``freeze_other_modes`` retains the spectator occupations unchanged.
No target occupation, frequency, or Hamiltonian coefficient is captured.
